Crawler AI Installer

Set up Crawler AI on this computer

Four steps, all buttons. The first install takes 10–20 minutes, mostly downloading.

The installer window was closed Double-click the Crawler AI installer again to pick up where you left off.
  1. 1

    Check your computer

    Crawler AI runs inside Docker Desktop (free for personal use). This checks that it’s installed and running, and that there’s room to work.

    On Windows, Docker Desktop needs WSL 2 (Windows Subsystem for Linux). Its installer turns WSL 2 on for you; restart when it asks, then open Docker Desktop once.

    • 2

      Security keys

      Two secret keys protect your install: one signs sign-ins, the other encrypts the credentials you save. They go into backend/.env on this computer.

      How to create the keys

      At least 32 characters with no spaces, quotes, backslashes, $ or #.

      Base64 of exactly 32 bytes: 44 characters ending in =. Standard or URL-safe alphabet.

      The keys never leave this computer. The installer doesn’t show them again and never writes them to its log.

    • 3

      Build & start

      • The first build downloads about 3.6 GB (database, Python and Node images plus Crawler AI’s dependencies).
      • Expect 5–20 minutes depending on your connection. Later starts take seconds.
      • You can leave this page open and do something else while it runs.
