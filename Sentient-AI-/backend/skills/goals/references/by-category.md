# Goals by category

Notes for the goals skill. Each category lists the questions that most often
shape a good goal (asked one per turn, only when the answer is not already
known), the sources Crawler may draw on once the goal is clear, and a few
small first milestones. Data sources are suggested only after the shape of
the goal is settled, and only the ones the user has connected.

## School

- Questions: which course or exam, the grade or result wanted, the date it is
  decided, and how many hours a week are realistic.
- Sources: canvas.get_upcoming for due work, canvas.grade_whatif for what a
  target grade needs (quoted as an estimate), study.progress for reviews due.
- First milestones: "Finish the chapter 4 problem set by Thursday", "Make 30
  flashcards for unit 2", "Book office hours this week".
- Note: Crawler helps the student learn and plan; it does not produce graded
  work for them.

## Career

- Questions: the role or field, the deadline (application season, graduation),
  and what already exists (resume, portfolio, contacts).
- Sources: web.research for postings and requirements, the user's own files
  when they point to them.
- First milestones: "Update the resume with the summer job", "List 10 companies
  hiring interns", "Ask one professor for a reference".
- Note: Crawler drafts outreach messages for the user to review; nothing goes
  out without the user's approval card.

## Health

- Questions: the habit or result wanted, current routine, time available, and
  anything that limits activity.
- Sources: what the user reports; no clinical records are read without the
  user asking.
- First milestones: "Walk 20 minutes three days this week", "Be in bed by
  midnight on weeknights".
- Note: general habits only. No diagnosis, no medication advice, no extreme
  diets or punishing schedules. Pain, injury or anything medical goes to a
  doctor or campus health service.

## Money

- Questions: the amount and date, current monthly income and fixed costs (in
  round numbers), and where the savings will sit.
- Sources: numbers the user gives; statements only when the user shares them.
- First milestones: "Track spending for one week", "Move $25 into savings on
  payday", "Cancel one unused subscription".
- Note: budgeting and saving habits only. No personalised investment, tax or
  legal advice, and Crawler moves no money for a goal.

## Personal

- Questions: what success looks like in the user's words, how often, and what
  got in the way before.
- First milestones: "Read 20 pages on Sunday", "Call home every Saturday",
  "Practise guitar 15 minutes after dinner".
- Note: people mentioned in a goal stay part of that goal only; Crawler builds
  no profiles of them.

## Other

- Used when no category fits. The same intake applies: the outcome, how it
  will be measured, the date, and one first step.
